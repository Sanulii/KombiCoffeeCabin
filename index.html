The Van Has a Story

Sam bought the Kombi at an estate sale for way too much money, gutted the back seats, and built in a proper 2-group espresso machine. What started as weekend farmers-market pours turned into a daily corner spot with a line before 8am.

Everything's small-batch: beans roasted 20 minutes from where the van parks, milk from a family dairy up the road, and a rotating pastry case baked by whoever in Sam's crew felt like baking that morning.
